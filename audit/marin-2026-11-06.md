## A 262K test avoids a memory failure when switching to evaluation (2026-11-06)

A 262K test completed training and evaluation on 11 racks after a change kept model pieces on their existing devices during the switch. This removes one source of extra memory use that had stopped a one-rack test. The earlier switch created communication buffers of 5.63 GiB and the next step ran out of memory. In the patched test, 100 steps ran across 704 devices, with two handoffs and a final evaluation. The handoff added no memory outside the allocator pool. The first evaluation computation still added about 1.03 GiB per device in communication buffers, and the tightest device had 2.08 GiB free. The fix merged in #10994. The test covers the reported configuration and leaves main-run adoption and longer-run stability open.
