## Timing: representative sampling changes the interpretation

[September 29 follow-up in #9205](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/9205#issuecomment-5901019798), posted 23:28 UTC, follows yesterday's review. The earlier 141 FineLog samples were all every-tenth-step diagnostic/watch updates. They were not representative of all training updates. The follow-up uses unsampled W&B history and reports:

- Mean full-iteration minus training-duration gap **0.335 s**, **2.2%** of a 15.21 s iteration for the specified window, excluding the evaluation at step 176999.
- Paired median gap **0.869 s** on diagnostic updates, **0.230 s** on ordinary updates and **3.114 s** on coordinated-GC updates. The earlier prose estimate of 0.77 s should not replace these more explicit paired statistics.
- The excluded evaluation adds about 58 seconds once every 3000 updates, roughly 0.02 seconds per update when amortized.
- Across steps 171469–179469, **8001 updates**, no trailing 12-interval median timestamp excess exceeds 1.2 seconds. About 1.4% of intervals exceed that threshold individually, versus roughly 40% in the older September 16 comparison. The historical recurring pattern is not observed in this newer window; its cause is not thereby proven.

The public report describes 371 thread-dump rounds, 254509 rank samples and exclusion of 2460 startup/missing-thread samples. It estimates mean per-update contributions around 0.13 seconds for router-metric conversion, 0.10 seconds for checkpoint-decision broadcast and 0.04 seconds for diagnostic logging, with rarer GC/evaluation costs. Stack sampling estimates attribution; linked internal FineLog raw records were not independently accessed. The report's public narrative and source links are the evidence, not an independently reproduced profile. Do not conflate present host overhead with the September 27 stop or older multi-rack GPU hangs.

[PR #9600](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/9600) merged **September 30 16:48:20 UTC**. It starts copying JAX-array leaves from GPU to host before converting individual values. The one-GB200 component test reports **175→58 ms** for 828 leaves and **740→269 ms** for 3592 leaves, medians of 120 trials. Suppressed/non-primary trackers still materialize values as required. This is a confirmed repository change and experimental component result. No launch/handoff or measured eleven-rack benefit was found. Whole-run time savings cannot be read directly from component percentages.
