## The selected recipe lowers long-document loss in the offline comparison (2026-11-09)

In the offline comparison, the selected recipe predicted answers better on a long-document test. The general held-out text score was effectively tied, and a control that removed the key evidence still showed most of the loss difference. At step 393491, full-context MRCR negative log likelihood was 0.0223 versus 0.0252 (difference −0.0029, 95% interval −0.0037 to −0.0021). Distractor-only loss fell by 0.0025. Recovery was 0.982 versus 0.981, with an interval crossing zero. The 19-subset 262K clean macro was 1.6434 versus 1.6433. There were 299 MRCR documents and one training seed per recipe. The intervals quantify document sampling, leaving run-to-run training variation and a retrieval-specific gain unresolved.
