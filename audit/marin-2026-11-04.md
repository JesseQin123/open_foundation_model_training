### The one-rack daily check timed out before training started

The new Hero canary waited for a full rack and ran out of workflow time before it could take a training step. The failure exposes a monitoring gap and provides no measurement of training speed. Issue #10897 reports that the child job was labeled RUNNING while all 16 training tasks were still waiting for Kueue admission. The monitor dropped its five-hour queue deadline and the workflow later timed out at 340 minutes. All 679 task-state samples in the cited interval showed 16 building, zero assigned and one running coordinator. The metric report never ran. The issue remains open and calls for keeping the queue deadline active until training work starts.

### A CPU export matched GPU output on a slice of a Hero checkpoint

A CPU test reproduced the exported weights for part of a Hero checkpoint byte for byte. This suggests CPUs can take over some export work while full-model export time and serving parity remain open. The merged #10890 reports 55 shards totaling 70.8 GB from step 378000, covering ordinary weights and one six-layer expert chunk. The test took 487.6 seconds, peaked at 95.6 GiB resident memory and used zero GPUs. All shard hashes matched the GPU export. The tool targets checkpoint branches in an existing private repository. The public PR establishes this scoped read and test, with complete publication and serving validation still outstanding.

### Marin accepted a device layout change for future longer-context tests

A merged code change arranges devices so evaluation can reuse communication groups that training already uses.
