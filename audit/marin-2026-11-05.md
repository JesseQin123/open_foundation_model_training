## An attention change for 262K passes a small test

Marin ran a small test of a change meant to help the model choose which words to focus on in very long documents. The change passed a forward-and-backward check on one GB200 node at 4K tokens, and merged as an optional diagnostic with the main Hero configuration leaving it off. The mean output-score difference between splitting the input across four devices and keeping it on one was 0.00045 with scaling and 0.00042 without it. The test shows that the modified operation ran in that setup. Quality, speed and memory at the intended 262K shape remain open.
