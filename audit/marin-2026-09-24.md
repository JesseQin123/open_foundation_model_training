## Save stall, restart and current measurements

The [new incident comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/8870#issuecomment-5804993083), published September 24 at 00:03 UTC, reports a September 23 stall inside rank 0's S3 `put_object` call for checkpoint 146585's `manifest.json`. Two host-thread captures showed the same stack. This localizes the observed wait; why the storage request remained blocked is unresolved. No CUDA debugger was attached, and this differs from earlier rack-wide GPU captures. Do not attribute the event to the newly deployed attention code or claim it solves the earlier hang diagnosis.

The watchdog terminated the attempt. The scheduler started attempt 1, restored temporary checkpoint 146355 at 00:01:55 UTC and resumed training. The report states a rollback of 228 steps from the last observed metric. That confirms recovery, not a permanent fix. [PR #9395](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/9395) separately proposes narrowing checkpoint deletion restrictions and remains open; it does not establish the cause of a blocked upload or prove that cause is fixed.

Its production adoption is established by the handoff above. Yesterday's historical combined-candidate description is retained with its original check time.

The separated [symmetric-buffer PR #9374](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/9374) remains open/draft. Its latest paired one-rack study reports 0.09% mean throughput improvement, with a 95% bootstrap interval from -0.17% to +0.27%. It has not established a dependable average benefit; full Hero collective lowering and interleaved evaluation remain untested.
